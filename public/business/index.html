Miami & South Florida

Starting in South Florida? You're at our front door.

Miami is one of our three U.S. logistics points. Units headed to South Florida are received, prepared and inspected here before delivery, which means a shorter final leg, a transport quote that doesn't cross the country, and the option to pick up locally instead of paying for long-haul.

  • Pick up in Miami instead of paying for cross-country transport.
  • Visits by appointment, subject to availability — most units are built to order, so there is no permanent showroom.
  • We work in Spanish and English, which matters when half your customers do too.
  • Campaigns targeted by ZIP, so you're bidding on the neighborhoods you can actually service.

Houston TX and Long Beach CA are our other logistics points. Wherever you operate, transport is quoted for your ZIP code — we don't estimate a mileage cost without checking.

Who rents restroom trailers here

The demand a South Florida operator typically quotes for:

  • Weddings and private events at venues without fixed restrooms
  • Beach, park and waterfront events
  • Construction and road crews on long jobs
  • Film and production shoots on location
  • Festivals, races and community events
  • Golf tournaments and corporate outings
  • Restroom overflow during building renovations
  • Storm recovery and emergency response

Which of these you win depends on your pricing, your service and how you market — this is the demand to go after, not demand we hand you.

Straight answers

Questions we get before anyone signs.

Is this a franchise?

No. There is no franchise agreement, no territory grant, no royalty and no franchise fee. You buy a trailer and, optionally, the services and technology that help you run a rental business around it. The business is yours, under your own name, and you set your own prices.

How much can I make?

We won't give you a number, because nobody honestly can. What we can give you are market references: a 2-Stall commonly rents around US$1,100/day, a 3-Stall around US$1,400–1,500/day and a 4-Stall around US$1,800/day. Those vary by location, season, what services you include and how much demand you generate.

Use the calculator with your own assumptions. It is a planning tool, not a projection, and NTG does not guarantee bookings, revenue or profit.

I'm not good with technology. Is that a problem?

It's the reason the product exists. You don't configure the CRM, build the website, write the automations or set up the ad accounts — that arrives assembled. Day to day you're looking at one dashboard: who wrote in, what needs a quote, what's booked, what's due.

On the Managed package, even most of that runs without you.

Do I need a truck?

For the Starter package it helps a lot — that's the version built for someone who tows and delivers personally. If you don't have one, or don't want to drive, delivery can be routed to a partner or coordinated for you on the higher packages.

Can I do this alongside a business I already run?

That's one of the most common cases. If you already do event rentals, porta-potties, party rentals, construction services or septic work, you have the customers and the logistics. Restroom trailers are a higher-ticket line you can sell to people who already call you.

How does financing work?

Pre-qualification runs through Acorn Finance: about two minutes, no impact on your credit score, available up to US$100,000, and no VIN required upfront. Funds may be deposited 24–48 hours after approval.

We can't promise approval, a rate, a term or a monthly payment — that's between you and the lender. Check what you pre-qualify for →

Where is the trailer delivered?

Units are received, prepared and inspected at our logistics points — Miami FL, Houston TX and Long Beach CA. You can pick up there, or we help coordinate transport to your location. Transport is quoted for your ZIP; we don't guess a mileage cost.

What paperwork comes with the unit?

The applicable commercial and origin documentation, including a Bill of Sale and MCO/MSO and VIN documentation where applicable. Registration requirements and taxes vary by state and are handled and paid by you in your state. The unit is not delivered already registered, plated or titled.

What's the warranty?

12-month factory warranty on the unit.

Can I start with the Starter package and add the rest later?

Yes. Everything is modular on purpose. A lot of owners start owner-operated, learn their market for a season, and switch on automation, campaigns or full management once the calendar starts filling.

Can I come see a unit?

Most units are built to order, so there is no traditional showroom. Visits in Miami are by appointment and subject to availability; Texas and California are logistics and preparation points, not showrooms. When a unit is on the ground, a video walkthrough is often possible — ask and we'll tell you what's available.
